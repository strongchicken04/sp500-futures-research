0. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
==================================================================================================================
DECISION: C - FURTHER RESEARCH REQUIRED. A trading simulation (Stage 5) is NOT justified now.
(Pre-registered criteria: C1-C3 and C6, C8, C9 pass; C4 and C5 fail; C7 is flagged.)

1. Reproduction and discrepancy.
   - Stage 3 is reproduced exactly (RF PR-AUC 0.0596, P@1% 7.74% = 1,296/16,749).
   - The 1,296 vs 1,263 discrepancy is a model mix-up. 1,263/16,749 is the full-A GBM, because the Stage 3
     tradability routine was coded on GBM. Same rows, different model.
   - RF's own top-1% profile is even cheaper than the Stage 3 report said: median $0.46, 78% below $1.

2. Data revision.
   - QC revised its historical daily data mid-run. The identical code now builds 6,028,644 rows / 24,426 events
     instead of 6,022,604 / 24,371.
   - The run check caught it. It was diagnosed (not project-related) and documented.
   - It moves the Stage 3 metrics by <= 0.0005 PR-AUC.

3. Events.
   - Test events 2021-25: 1,539 distinct (E1), 511 securities, about 6 positive days per event.
   - RF top 1% / 5% / 10% captures 28.8% / 62.8% / 79.5% of events. But only 17% / 12.5% / 9.4% of signals
     belong to an event: about 31 unrelated signals per captured event at the top 1%.

4. Timing (confirmatory test failed).
   - The signal appears before onset in 57% of events, but the same stock was flagged 31-40 sessions earlier
     in 50% of events.
   - Ratio 1.14 (needed >= 1.5); the difference of +7.2 pp is real but small.
   - The window profile is flat: 1-2 sessions before onset 47%, 21-40 sessions before 56% (top 5%).
   - The model identifies a persistent stock state, not the timing of the move.

5. Deduplication.
   - Removes 84-88% of signals (one per 10 sessions) at a capture cost of 2-3 pp.
   - Does not improve precision (-0.8 pp at 5%), so conclusions are unchanged.

6. Tradability.
   - In the full universe, 78% of RF top-1% picks are below $1.
   - After filtering to liquid stocks and re-ranking, the ranking survives:
       px >= $1 and $vol >= $500k: lift 21.5x (validation 22.7x), 457 events, 76% captured at top 5%.
       px >= $5 and $vol >= $1M:   lift 41x.
   - Absolute top-1% precision is only 3.5-5.3%.
   - Minute quote data exist (about 88% of sampled days). Median quoted spread on the entry day after a signal
     is about 1.1% (first 30 minutes about 1.7%).

7. Downside (flagged).
   - The selected stocks fall on average from the next open: RF top 1% median close -4.1% / -7.1% / -12.4% at
     t+5 / t+10 / t+20; 63% are negative at t+10.
   - 8% lose half their value within 20 sessions; 14% touch a -50% low.
   - Even in the liquid segment, a -50% close at t+10 (3.8%) is more common than a +100% close (3.1%).

8. RF vs the simple volume model (paired tests).
   - RF is better in overall ranking (PR-AUC +0.0139 [0.0080, 0.0195], p < 0.001, robust to date clustering),
     at top 5-10% and in event capture (+12 pp at 5%).
   - At the very top 1% the two are statistically indistinguishable (+0.6 pp [-0.6, 1.7]).
   - The advantage comes from stocks >= $1. It holds in 2021-22, 2023-25 and 2026 YTD, in all market regimes,
     and after date clustering.

9. Robustness.
   - All conclusions are unchanged in A-strict, A-ex-ADR, A-fund and B.
   - The same holds for close-confirmed events (B, B-max), for E2 and E3, and when incomplete labels are counted
     as negatives.

Confounder (must be resolved before any confirmatory claim):
  - Segment membership is decided by price and dollar volume at t.
  - Dollar volume tends to rise before events, so a stock is more often outside the segment in the placebo

  - A proper test needs a membership-matched placebo, e.g. requiring the stock to be in the segment in both
    windows.
  - This makes "timing inside liquid segments" the most promising, but not yet established, line of further
    research.

=> DECISION C: FURTHER RESEARCH REQUIRED. A trading simulation is not justified on this evidence.

Interpretation: the model reliably finds stocks that are in a chronic extreme-volatility state. It does not
show WHEN they will move. Those stocks, including liquid ones, lose money on average from the next open.
Family-A excursions are often unheld intraday highs.

What a next research stage should test, pre-registered and before any simulation:
  (1) a timing target, e.g. a move within 1-3 sessions conditional on the high-volatility state, judged against
      the within-security placebo;
  (2) a capturable target, e.g. close or VWAP excursion with an adverse-move limit;
  (3) the work restricted to S1 / S2-type liquid stocks, where quote data allow a cost model;
  (4) Stage 5 only if (1)-(3) pass.

This report makes no profitability claim. A predictive ranking is not a trading strategy.
