1. BOTTOM LINE
--------------
* Survivorship: the QC US equity universe is point-in-time and includes later-bankrupt / delisted /
  acquired companies (10 of 10 known cases present before their exit; 62% of the 2008 universe no longer
  exists in 2026; 24,127 distinct securities seen 2006-2026). PASS.
* Price / volume (daily and minute, incl. pre-market and after-hours): available 2006-2026 for listed
  common stocks. PASS.
* Market capitalisation: point-in-time (checked across the AAPL 4:1 split and the GE 1:8 reverse split).
  PASS, with the caveat in 4.2.
* Shares outstanding and FLOAT fields: EXIST, but they are RESTATED FOR LATER SPLITS (not point-in-time
  with respect to raw prices). Usable only after a documented correction (section 4.3). Float exists only
  from 2010 (23% coverage in Jan 2010, 84-94% from 2011). Float hypotheses can therefore be tested only on
  2011-2026, and only with a derived ("corrected") float -> PARTIAL RESEARCH, labelled as such.
* Short interest: QC has FINRA short interest only from 2018-01 (the dataset page says 2017; the first
  record delivered is 2018-01-11). Values change on the FINRA publication schedule (around the 11th-13th
  and 26th-28th), i.e. publication-stamped, not settlement-stamped (section 5); an extra 1-day lag is applied
  anyway. Short-interest hypotheses can be tested only on 2018-2026 -> PARTIAL RESEARCH. No short interest
  before 2018 exists in QC.
* Trading halts: no halt dataset in QC. Not testable directly (a proxy is possible, labelled as a proxy).
* News / catalysts: Benzinga news class exists in QC (coverage dates to be confirmed in Stage 2); FDA /
  offerings / dilution only via news text or SEC filings -> optional, later stage.
* The project will NOT be downgraded to an easier question because of these gaps. The core hypotheses that
  are fully testable (market cap, RVOL, volatility, gaps, momentum, price level, liquidity, corporate actions)
  will be tested on 2006-2026; float and short interest will be tested on their own sub-periods and reported
  as PARTIAL tests of the main hypothesis, never as confirmation of it.

4. POINT-IN-TIME ISSUES FOUND (AND HOW THEY WILL BE HANDLED)
------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Prices: raw prices are used for the reference price and for share-count-based variables; split- and
    dividend-adjusted prices for returns. Reverse splits are corporate-action events in QC (verified for GE),
    so a reverse split does not create a fake +700% move in adjusted data. The labelling code will use adjusted
    highs relative to the adjusted reference close and will flag any event day that coincides with a split
    event for manual-rule exclusion.
4.2 Market cap: Morningstar MarketCap equals raw price x point-in-time shares (AAPL 2020-08-04: price 435.8,
    MC 1,863B -> 4.28B shares = the pre-split count; GE 2021-07-01: price 13.5, MC 118.2B -> 8.76B shares =
    the pre-reverse-split count). It is fill-forwarded (monthly share counts). It will be used as is, measured
    at the prediction close of the previous day.
4.3 Shares outstanding and float are restated for later splits:
    AAPL 2020-08-04 (before the 4:1 split): SharesOutstanding 17,103M, Float 17,093M (post-split numbers).
    GE 2021-07-01 (before the 1:8 reverse split): SharesOutstanding 1,098M, Float 1,091M (post-split numbers).
    Correction used in the study (documented, labelled "derived PIT float"):
        PIT shares        = MarketCap / raw price
        PIT float         = Float x (PIT shares / SharesOutstanding)
    i.e. the float / shares-outstanding RATIO from Morningstar is assumed to be point-in-time, and the share
    count is re-based to the split state of that date. Validation planned in Stage 2: the corrected float must
    jump only on genuine issuance / buyback / float-change dates, not on split dates.
    Caveat: Morningstar's float definition and update lag are not documented in QC. Float values change in
    lock-step with share-count updates (97% of stocks), so the float may lag real changes (e.g. lock-up
    expiries, offerings) by up to one Morningstar update cycle. This is a limitation of the float tests.
4.4 Fundamentals timing: Morningstar values are fill-forwarded and keyed to a file date (approximated as period
    end + 45 days for old filings). All fundamentals will be lagged one trading day beyond the QC delivery date.
4.5 Look-ahead fields excluded from predictors: DelistingDate, DelistingReason, ShareClassStatus, IPO flags
    that reflect the current state, current sector classification (sector kept only as a descriptive split).
4.6 Morningstar feed migration (2026-09-23): QC replaced the whole fundamental history with the new
    Morningstar Equity Data Feeds; ~1/3 of overlapping values changed. All runs of this project use the
    current (post-migration) data consistently; results are not comparable with pre-migration backtests.


5. SHORT INTEREST TIMING (STAGE 1c / 1d)
----------------------------------------
Stage 1c: the FINRA short-interest universe is delivered every calendar day from 2018-01-11 as a forward-
filled snapshot of 9,000-18,000 symbols (p10 9,243 / p50 14,066 / p90 17,896 records). Delivery dates are
uniform over the month, so they do not reveal the publication date. Record time = the previous day.
Stage 1d (value-change timing, 600 symbols, 2018-2026): data fields = short_interest, previous_short_interest,
change, days_to_cover, average_daily_volume, price. The dates on which the short-interest values change cluster
on the 11th-13th (25% of all changes) and the 26th-28th (25%) of each month; every other day of the month
carries only ~2% (the daily-changing 'price' field). FINRA settlement dates are mid-month and month-end; FINRA
disseminates ~8 business days later (around the 9th-12th and the 24th-27th). The observed change days match
the PUBLICATION schedule (0-2 days after it), not the settlement dates.
-> QC delivers FINRA short interest publication-stamped. No look-ahead from settlement dating was found.
Rule that will be applied regardless of the result: short interest for settlement date S is treated as
available only from FINRA's publication date (settlement + 8 business days, FINRA's standard dissemination
schedule) + 1 trading day. If QC already stamps the data at publication, this adds a conservative one-day lag;
if QC stamps it at settlement, this removes the look-ahead.


6. WHAT IS MISSING AND EXTERNAL OPTIONS
---------------------------------------
* Float before 2010 and an exact, documented float history: CRSP (shares outstanding only, no float),
  Compustat (shares, some float items), FactSet / Bloomberg / S&P Capital IQ (historical float, paid).
* Short interest before 2018: Compustat Supplemental Short Interest (NYSE/AMEX from the 1970s, Nasdaq from
  2003; via WRDS), Nasdaq Data Link / exchange files, ORTEX / S3 (paid, from ~2010s).
* Trading halts: Nasdaq Trader and NYSE publish halt / LULD histories (coverage to verify).
* Spreads: TAQ / quote data (paid) for true spreads; otherwise high-low spread estimators (proxy).
* Corporate catalysts (FDA, offerings, contracts): news (Benzinga in QC, to verify) or SEC EDGAR filings
  (S-1 / S-3 / 424B for offerings; 8-K items) - external parsing would be needed for EDGAR.
Note on the QC terms: QC flagged data exports earlier in this project. External data cannot be merged
security-by-security with QC data outside QC; anything external would have to be uploaded into QC as a
custom data source and the analysis kept inside QC.


7. CONSEQUENCES FOR THE RESEARCH DESIGN (PROPOSED FOR STAGE 2+)
---------------------------------------------------------------
Block A - FULL TEST, 2006-2026: market cap (PIT), RVOL (daily; intraday where feasible), ATR / price and
          realised volatility, gaps, momentum (1-20 days, distance from highs, streaks), price level, dollar
          volume / liquidity, corporate-action flags, market regime. Chronological split: train 2006-2016,
          validation 2017-2020, untouched test 2021-2026, with purge / embargo of 20 sessions at each boundary.
Block B - PARTIAL TEST, 2011-2026: derived PIT float, float turnover, volume / float. Split: train 2011-2017,
          validation 2018-2020, test 2021-2026. Reported as a partial test of the low-float hypothesis.
Block C - PARTIAL TEST, 2018-2026: short interest % of (derived) float, days to cover, change in SI, with the
          publication-lag rule of section 5. Split: train 2018-2021, test 2022-2026 (no separate validation;
          stated as a limitation). Reported as a partial test of the short-interest hypothesis.
Block D - OPTIONAL: news / catalyst flags if Benzinga coverage proves adequate.
Event labels (all blocks): max(adjusted high over the next 5 / 10 / 20 sessions) / reference close - 1 >= 50 /
100 / 200%, plus close-based versions; reference = the close of the prediction day (prediction made after the
close, entry no earlier than the next open). Event clustering: a new event for the same stock only after the
previous window ends (non-overlapping by stock), and inference with stock-level clustering / block bootstrap.
Compute: everything runs inside QC (daily universe ~4,000-5,500 common stocks with fundamentals); predictors
and labels are built in-QC and models (logistic, regularised logistic, random forest / gradient boosting via
scikit-learn in QC) are fit in-QC; only aggregate statistics leave QC.

Next step: Stage 2 - build and validate the research universe on random dates (type filters, exchange mix,
delisting-return handling, SPAC heuristic, derived-float validation across split dates).
