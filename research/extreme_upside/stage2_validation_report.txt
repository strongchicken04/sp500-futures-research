1.5 Universes and what each can answer
A  broad price/volume: every traded security (6.7k-11.1k a day). Contains ETFs and ETNs, which cannot be
   separated from stocks without fundamentals, plus warrants, units, rights and test symbols. Not usable as is.
   A-stock = A minus W/U/R/'.' tickers, minus 5-letter Y/F, minus test symbols, minus a POINT-IN-TIME ETF/ETN
   list. That list does not exist in QC and would have to be IMPORTED into QC (import, not export; e.g. built
   from SEC fund filings / exchange listing files). With it, A-stock is the survivorship-complete universe for
   price, volume, gap, momentum and volatility hypotheses. It would hold the ~1/3 of stock events that B misses.
   Without the list, A-stock can only be a robustness check with ~18% fund contamination among no-fundamental
   names (V1c sample).
B  fundamental core (corrected as in 1.3, test symbols removed): 2.8k (2006) - 4.2k (2026) plus 250-400 restored
   US stocks. Main universe for every hypothesis that needs market cap or shares (2006-2026). It must always be
   reported with its selection bias: it misses about a third of stock events (1.4).
C  B with float: 2011-2026 only (no float before 2010, 23% in Jan 2010). Float hypotheses are PARTIAL and use
   an uncertain variable (section 4).
D  B with short interest: 2018-01 - 2026-08 only, 99% of B. Short-interest hypotheses are PARTIAL
   (8.6 years, one market regime family).

Defensible treatment (fixed before testing):
* Upside labels are computed only on real sessions (fill-forward OFF).
* A window that runs past the last valid session is INCOMPLETE. Family A (max excursion) is evaluated on the
  available sessions and flagged. For family B (close-based), the window is dropped from the main count;
  sensitivity variants use the last price, last x 0.7 (Shumway-type performance-delisting return) and zero.
* A series is split into separate segments at any gap longer than 10 market sessions. No window crosses a
  segment boundary (see 6.3: ticker reuse splices companies).

  -> For UPSIDE labels the delisting assumption changes event counts by less than 1%. It matters for

* On raw prices every reverse split is a fake +600% to +2,900% "event". In the V5 sample, 1,599 of 8,781
  raw +100%/10d events (18%) and 3,375 of 8,708 raw +200%/20d events (39%) are artificial (raw yes, adjusted
  no). Adjusted-only events not on raw: 10 and 7.
* Spin-offs are carried as split factors (GE 2023, 2024), so the adjusted series has no artificial drop.
* Dividend adjustment hardly matters for these thresholds: total-return-adjusted vs split-only-adjusted
  family-A events differ in 10 of 7,192 cases (+100%/10d) and 3 of 5,340 (+200%/20d).
* Hazard: adjusted LEVELS can be absurd. After four reverse splits, MULN's adjusted close for 2023-05-03 is
  5.4e15. Ratios stay exact (the label equals raw where no split falls in the window), but adjusted levels must
  never be used as features.
* With the open(t+1) reference, a split exactly at t+1 creates no fake event even on raw data (MULN example:
  raw = adjusted +14%). A close(t) reference on raw prices would.

ONE CONVENTION (fixed):
* Returns, labels, highs and gaps: QC ADJUSTED (split + dividend + spin-off factor). Ratios only, never levels.
* Price levels and tradability (price < $1, $1-5, dollar volume): RAW, as of t.
* Corporate-action features: from LEAN Split and Dividend events, usable from the ex-date (warning event on t-1).


4. DERIVED POINT-IN-TIME FLOAT (V3)
-----------------------------------
DS = MarketCap / raw price (PIT share count). Derived PIT float = Float x DS / SO. Universe: fundamental common
stocks, 2011-01 .. 2026-08.

4.1 PIT share count DS: VALIDATED
* Agreement with the independent estimate SO x factor-file split factor (January snapshots):
  2011 88% within 5%, 94% within 20%, 4% off by more than 1.5x; 2016 90/95/3; 2021 90/96/3; 2026 90/95/3.
* Splits detected from factor-file ex-dates: 909 forward, 2,427 reverse (2,052 reverse splits below $1). DS jumps
  by the split ratio ON THE EX-DATE in 83% (forward), 82% (reverse) and 85% (low-priced reverse) of cases. Lag of
  1-30 days: 1-3%. Not within 30 days: 13-15%. In those cases market cap or SO is out of sync and the derived float
  is wrong by up to the split ratio. This is detectable in real time from the DS jump and can be flagged.
* Issuance and buybacks are captured: AMC DS 104M (2020-06) -> 216M (2021-01) -> 548M (2021-07) -> 563M
  (2022-07); GME 65M -> 74M -> 76M -> 305M after the 4:1 split; AAPL 929M (2012) -> 5,575M (2016, after 7:1)
  -> 4,443M (2020) -> 15,553M (2024, after 4:1). These match the known share counts.
* PIT discipline: earning_reports.file_date is never in the future (0.00% of 3.64M weekly observations). SO
  changes come 7-99 days after the latest filing (p10 / p90), never before it.

4.2 The float ratio (Float / SO): NOT VALIDATED, uncertain
* Staleness: 96% of float changes happen in weeks with an SO change, but only 22% of SO changes change the
  Float / SO ratio. The float is mostly the SO times a ratio that is updated about once in five share updates.
  Days between float changes: p25 28, p50 41, p90 91.
* Inconsistent bases:
  - AMC: derived float 4M (2020-06), 16M, 55M (2021-07) against DS 104-548M (4-10%). This is implausible.
    SO x split factor is also off 20x for AMC, so Float and SO were restated on different bases around the
    2023 1:10 split and APE conversion.
  - GME: 22M of 65M (2020-06) and 44M of 74M (2021-07). Morningstar's float excludes large holders. That is a
    definitional difference from the "float" used in squeeze narratives, not necessarily an error.
* The age-of-float snapshot (p50 0 days on 2016-01-04 and 2024-01-02) is dominated by a mass refresh in early
  January and is inconclusive.
DECISION: PIT shares (DS) is a validated variable from 2006. Derived float is an UNCERTAIN DERIVED VARIABLE, used
only in Universe C, flagged (ratio age, post-split DS mismatch, float / DS < 0.1 implausible), and reported as
partial evidence. A float-based result cannot confirm the main hypothesis.


5. SHORT-INTEREST TIMESTAMPS (V4, V4b)
--------------------------------------
* FINRA rule: settlement on the 15th (or the previous settlement day) and at month end; reports due 2 business
  days later; publication 7 business days after settlement (FINRA Short Interest Reporting page, 2026 schedule
  example: Jan 15 settlement -> Jan 27 publication). The rule calendar was rebuilt from NYSE sessions.
  www.finra.org is blocked from this environment, so the archived 2021 table could not be fetched. Tolerance:
  +-1 day around DTC holidays on which NYSE trades.
* QC record: time = day D 00:00, end_time = D+1 00:00, delivered to the algorithm at D+1 00:00 (usable in the
  session of the first trading day >= end_time date). There is no settlement-date field.
* Main cycle changes vs rule publication (usable session minus publication, in trading days):
    2021 (GME / AMC / AAPL / TSLA / BBBY, identical): rule 01-12 01-27 02-09 02-24 03-09 03-24 04-12 04-26
                                                     QC   01-13 01-28 02-11 02-25 03-11 03-26 04-13 04-28
                                                     -> +1 +1 +2 +1 +2 +2 +1 +2
    2018 Q1: rule 01-24 02-09 02-27 03-09 03-26 / QC 01-25 02-13 02-28 03-13 03-28 -> +1 +2 +1 +2 +2
    2022 Q1: rule 01-11 01-26 02-09 02-25 03-09 03-24 / QC 01-13 01-27 02-11 02-26(Sat -> Mon 02-28) -> +2 +1 +2 +1
  Over all value changes, n = 1 for 19.3% and n = 2 for 29.7%. The rest are off-cycle changes (below).
* VERDICT on "publication + 1 trading day": QC's timestamp is never earlier than publication + 1 trading day for
  cycle changes. It is +1 or +2. Use the QC end_time as the availability time and add NO extra delay.
  Stage 1's extra 1-day lag is removed.
* Off-cycle changes: about 45% of value changes fall on other days, spread evenly (-5..+11 trading days).
  previous_short_interest equals the prior value in only 36.8% of changes.
  V4b explains them. They are DUPLICATE RECORDS, not timing:
  - 495,814 cases of the same symbol appearing more than once in one daily file.
  - 93% of off-cycle changes are flip-backs (the value returns to the one before), i.e. two records
    alternating.
  - Off-cycle changes occur in only 9% of the 3,982 tracked symbols, and the top 5% of symbols hold 78% of them
    (CADC, IAM, KBSF, LBIX, ENB, EBIO, CCCR, CUR, ESDI, HR, GXF, FLEH; also the reused ticker BBBY: 465).
  - GME, AMC, AAPL and TSLA: 0 off-cycle changes.
  - They grow from 14k (2018) to 99k (2025) a year.
  Treatment: a symbol-day with more than one record is AMBIGUOUS and set to missing. Never pick one of the two
  values silently.
* Staleness / forward fill: the universe is a daily forward-filled snapshot. 22.4% of symbol-days carry a value
  unchanged for more than 35 days (21.5% more than 70 days, i.e. at least 2 missed cycles). si <= 0: 0.00%. One
  symbol had a 211-day delivery gap. Treatment: value age = days since the last change; age > 35 days -> missing,
  not forward-filled.
* Missing: 99% of B has a record on the sampled dates (2018-2026).

6.3 Label-integrity rules (fixed)
1. fill_forward = False for every history request.
2. Segment a series at gaps longer than 10 market sessions. No window crosses a segment boundary. Windows with
   1-10 missing sessions (halts, suspensions) are flagged, not dropped.
3. Labels on ADJUSTED ratios; open(t+1) reference; A and B both kept.
4. Incomplete windows flagged; A on available sessions as a sensitivity only; B dropped in the main count.
5. Zero-volume days inside a window are flagged (4% of +100%/10d events).

8. LIMITATIONS (stated openly)
------------------------------
* Data end 2026-08-27 in this environment.
* has_fundamental_data looks static per security. The coverage onboarding lag cannot be measured, and coverage
  may be assigned retroactively.
* Stocks without fundamentals (legacy lines of renamed companies, small foreign ordinaries, uncovered small
  caps): about 2/3 of the no-fundamental <= 4-letter event names. They cannot be separated from ETFs and ETNs
  inside QC (no fund flag), and the manual 48-ticker classification is mine. Without an imported PIT ETF list,
  Universe A stays contaminated and B stays biased.
* Exchange test symbols (ZVV, ZBZX, CBO ...) are in the QC universe and must be excluded explicitly.
* is_primary_share is unreliable for US single-class stocks. The corrected rule (1.3) is a heuristic.
* Morningstar migrated feeds on 2026-09-23 and restated history (QC documentation). The point-in-time checks
  (file_date never in the future, DS on split ex-dates) passed on the current data, but the restatement itself
  cannot be audited.
* Derived float: not validated (section 4.2).
* FINRA 2021 official calendar not fetched (site blocked). The rule calendar has +-1 day tolerance. Off-cycle
  short-interest changes are only partly explained (V4b).
* OTC continuation prices after exchange delisting are not in QC. SIVB-type halted exits have no market price.
* Ticker reuse splices companies in history() (BBBY). Handled by gap segmentation, but splices with short gaps
  would not be caught. The rate is unknown; 387 of 7,192 +100%/10d windows contain a missing session.
* Trading halts: a minute-bar proxy only. There is no official halt history in QC.
* SPACs: name/price heuristic plus Morningstar code 10350010. There is no explicit SPAC flag.

11.8 Final universe definitions for Stage 3 (fixed)
A        MAIN: all QC securities not identified as ETF / ETN / CEF / preferred / debt / warrant / right / unit /
         test.
         - Includes common stock with or without fundamentals, secondary share classes, ADR / foreign ordinaries
           and UNRESOLVED.
         - Every result is also reported for A-strict (no UNRESOLVED), A-ex-ADR, and A with the PIT fund-like flag
           applied to UNRESOLVED.
B        Morningstar fundamental common stocks with the primary-share correction (section 1.3), for market cap,
         shares, valuation and other fundamental hypotheses.
C        B with derived float (2011-), partial and uncertain (section 4).
D        A with a usable FINRA short-interest value (2018-01 .. 2026-08), publication timestamp, age <= 35 days,
         no duplicate-record days (section 5).
Mandatory label rules for all: fill-forward off, segment at gaps > 10 sessions, adjusted ratios, open(t+1)
reference, families A and B, incomplete windows flagged, non-overlapping episodes.

11.9 What is still not perfect
* 5,037 UNRESOLVED securities (34% of A by count, 30% of A events), concentrated in 2006-09 dead securities.
* Estimated non-equity share among them ~20-25%. They cannot be resolved with public historical data reachable
  from this environment.
* A few known misclassifications (OPWV as unit, TVC as common). The inclusion error on the inspected sample is
  ~2-3%.
* ETN coverage is current-only: 37 ETNs alive or traceable. Dead ETNs (e.g. the 2010s iPath / VelocityShares
  series) stay UNRESOLVED unless N-CEN or a name matched them. Most would be flagged fund-like only when calm.
* Morningstar coverage itself is static per security (section 1.4).
* The universe is therefore complete with respect to QC's survivorship-free security list, but its equity / non-
  equity split is only approximately complete before 2010 and nearly complete after 2018.
